SUSHANT PRINTS — COUNTER & LABELLING SYSTEM
Setup and daily-use guide


WHAT THIS SYSTEM DOES

Three jobs, one app.

  1. Generate QR — you make labels for a batch of pieces, print them, and stick
     one on each saree. Every label carries a unique code, its design name, the
     rate, and your shop name.

  2. Scan — at the counter you scan each piece a customer is buying. The
     scans collect into a cart. You hit Bill, and it makes one bill listing every
     piece, marks those pieces sold, and prints. Customer details are optional.

  3. Order form — a typed order for when scanning is not possible (a phone
     order, or the scanner is down). You type the party and the design lines by
     hand and it prints an order docket. This one does NOT touch stock. It is a
     fallback, not the main way to sell.

Because every piece is tracked from the moment you print its label, the system
also tells you what is still in stock and what has sold. That is your inventory,
for free. Bills keeps both kinds of order side by side: scanned bills (which move
stock) and typed order forms (which do not), each clearly tagged.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PART 1 — GETTING IT ONLINE (one time, about 20 minutes)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

You need three free things: a place to run the app (Render), a place to keep the
data (Neon), and later, your own web address. Do them in this order.


STEP 1 — PUT THE CODE ON GITHUB

  1. Make a free account at github.com if you do not have one.
  2. Make a new repository, name it anything, for example sushant-orders.
  3. Upload all the files from this folder into it (server.js, package.json,
     package-lock.json, render.yaml, and the public folder).


STEP 2 — MAKE THE DATABASE (Neon)

This is where every label and bill is stored. It is permanent and free.

  1. Go to neon.tech and sign up with your GitHub account.
  2. Create a project. Name it sushant. For region pick Singapore, it is closest.
  3. Neon shows you a "connection string". It looks like:
         postgresql://user:password@ep-something.neon.tech/neondb
     Copy the whole thing. If Neon offers a "pooled" string, copy that one.
     Keep it safe, it is the key to your data.


STEP 3 — RUN THE APP (Render)

  1. Go to render.com and sign up with GitHub.
  2. Click New, then Web Service. Connect the repository from Step 1.
  3. Render reads render.yaml and fills most settings in. Confirm:
         Build command:  npm install
         Start command:  node server.js
  4. Open the Environment section and add these values:

         DATABASE_URL   the connection string you copied from Neon
         APP_PASSWORD   the password your staff will type to sign in
         ADMIN_PIN      a 4-digit PIN, needed to cancel a bill or delete a piece
         CHANGE_PIN     a 4-digit PIN for protected settings

  5. Click Create. First build takes a few minutes. When it says Live, open the
     web address Render gives you (something.onrender.com) and sign in.

If the log ever says "DATABASE_URL not set", the database value in Step 4 is
missing or wrong. Fix it and Render restarts on its own.


STEP 4 — YOUR OWN WEB ADDRESS (do this BEFORE printing real labels)

This step matters. Every QR label points at a web address. If you print labels on
the free Render address and later move, every printed label stops working.

  So: buy a domain first. A .in address is about 700 to 900 rupees a year from
  GoDaddy, Hostinger, or BigRock. In Render, open Settings, then Custom Domain,
  add your domain, and follow the steps it shows. Once your domain opens the app,
  you are safe to print labels for real.

Until then, feel free to practise: generate a test batch, scan, bill, but treat
those labels as scrap.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PART 2 — USING IT EVERY DAY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

MAKING LABELS (Generate QR)

  1. Open Generate QR.
  2. Pick or type the design. If the design has a saved rate it fills in for you.
  3. Set the rate for this batch. The rate can be different each time, even for
     the same design, that is fine.
  4. Enter how many pieces.
  5. Choose a label size (small fits 18 to a sheet, medium 10, large 4).
  6. Click Generate and print labels. A print sheet opens. Print it, cut, and
     stick one label on each piece.

Every label is its own unique code, so no two pieces are ever mixed up. You can
reprint any past batch from the Recent batches list.


SELLING AT THE COUNTER

  1. Open Counter (on a phone this is the first screen, called Sell).
  2. Tap Scan a piece. The camera opens and keeps scanning. Point at each piece
     one by one, the cart fills as you go. Or type a code and press Add.
  3. When every piece is in the cart, tap Done to review.
  4. Add a discount if you are giving one.
  5. Tap Bill and print. If the customer wants a proper bill, type their name and
     details, otherwise leave it blank for a quick bill. Confirm, and the bill
     prints with every piece listed.

A piece already sold cannot be scanned again, the system stops it. So the same
saree can never be billed twice.


FINDING AND CANCELLING BILLS

  Bills lists every order. Tap one to see it in full, reprint it, or cancel it.
  Cancelling asks for the admin PIN and puts every piece on that bill back into
  stock so it can be sold again.


STOCK

  Stock shows every piece and where it is: in stock, sold, or cancelled. Filter
  by design or status to see what is left on the floor.


REPORTS

  Pick a date range and Run report. You get pieces sold, sales totals, a
  breakdown by design, and a breakdown by party. Download CSV saves it to open
  in Excel.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PART 3 — BACKUP (optional but recommended)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The app can copy each day's bills into a Google Sheet automatically at midnight.
To switch it on you add three more values in Render's Environment section:

    GOOGLE_CLIENT_EMAIL     from a Google service account
    GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY      from the same service account
    GOOGLE_SHEET_ID         the id of a Google Sheet you share with that account

Setting up a service account is a Google Cloud task. If that is more than you
want to handle, skip it. The Reports screen lets you download a CSV any time, and
that is a perfectly good manual backup.

On Render's free plan the app sleeps when idle, so the midnight backup only runs
if the app happens to be awake. Until you move to a paid plan, treat the CSV
download as your real backup.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GOOD TO KNOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  - The free Render plan sleeps after about 15 minutes of no use. The next visit
    takes up to a minute to wake. Paid plans stay awake, about 7 dollars a month.

  - Your staff stay signed in across sleeps and restarts, they will not have to
    log in over and over.

  - Never use Render's own free database, it deletes itself after 30 days. Always
    use Neon as described. Neon's free database is permanent.

  - The camera only works on a secure web address (https). Render and your custom
    domain both provide that, so it works in the browser with no app to install.
    On iPhone use Safari, on Android use Chrome, and allow the camera when asked.

  - To change the sign-in password, edit APP_PASSWORD in Render and let it
    redeploy.
