================================================================
SUSHANT PRINTS — ORDER AND LABELLING SYSTEM
Setup guide v1.0
================================================================

WHAT THIS DOES
--------------
Book an order with the customer details and the design lines.
The system gives it an order number like SP-2607-0042, prints an
A4 order form with a QR code on it, and keeps the whole record.

Anyone in the factory can then scan that QR on a phone and see the
full order on screen: party, mobile, agent, every design with its
quantity and remarks, the master quantity, and the current status.
They can move the status along from the same screen.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
A GitHub account, a Railway account, and about fifteen minutes.
This runs on the same setup as the stock system, so if you have
done that one before, none of this will be new.

================================================================
STEP 1 — PUT THE CODE ON GITHUB
================================================================
1. Go to https://github.com/new
2. Repository name: sushant-order-system
3. Set it to Private
4. Click Create repository
5. On the next screen click "uploading an existing file"
6. Drag in all of these:
     server.js
     package.json
     railway.toml
     public/          (the whole folder)
7. Click Commit changes

================================================================
STEP 2 — ADD THE DATABASE ON RAILWAY
================================================================
This is the step that keeps your orders safe. Skip it and the
data disappears every time the app redeploys.

1. Go to https://railway.app
2. Open your project, or create a new one
3. Click "+ New", then Database, then Add PostgreSQL
4. Railway sets DATABASE_URL for you. Nothing else to configure.

================================================================
STEP 3 — DEPLOY THE APP
================================================================
1. In the Railway project click "+ New", then GitHub Repo
2. Pick sushant-order-system
3. Wait three or four minutes for the build to go green

================================================================
STEP 4 — SET YOUR PASSWORD AND PINS
================================================================
1. Click the app service box in Railway
2. Open the Variables tab
3. Add these four:

     APP_PASSWORD   your sign-in password, for example sushant2026
     SESSION_SECRET any long random text, for example k9x2mq74nz
     ADMIN_PIN      four digits, needed to delete an order
     CHANGE_PIN     four digits, kept for sensitive changes

4. Click Deploy

Keep ADMIN_PIN with the owner only. It is the one thing standing
between a wrong tap and a deleted order.

================================================================
STEP 5 — GET YOUR LINK
================================================================
1. Click the app service, then Settings
2. Under Networking click Generate Domain
3. You get something like sushant-order-system.up.railway.app
4. Save this link and bookmark it on every phone in the factory

This link matters more than usual here. The QR codes on your
printed order forms point at it. If you ever change the domain,
old printed forms will stop opening, so pick one and keep it.

================================================================
STEP 6 — FIRST RUN
================================================================
1. Open the link in Chrome and sign in
2. Go to Settings and check the letterhead details are right.
   The company name, address, GST and phone numbers are already
   filled in from your card. They print at the top of every form.
3. Go to Designs and parties, add your regular design names.
   You can also just type a new design name while booking an
   order and it gets added to the list on its own.
4. Book a test order and print it
5. Open the Scan QR tab on a phone, scan the printed form, and
   check the whole order comes up

================================================================
HOW THE DAY RUNS
================================================================
Booking      New order tab. Fill Part A, add design lines in
             Part B, put in the master quantity, then Save and
             print order form.

Repeat party Start typing the party name. If they have ordered
             before, the address, mobile, GST, transport and
             agent fill in by themselves. Change whatever is
             different and carry on.

On the floor Scan QR tab, open the camera, point it at the form.
             The order opens with every detail on one screen.
             Use Update status to move it along:
             Pending, In Process, Ready, Delivered.

Looking up   Orders tab. Search by order number, party name,
             mobile, or even a design name.

Month end    Reports tab. Pick the dates, run the report, then
             Download CSV.

================================================================
ORDER NUMBERS
================================================================
They look like SP-2607-0042.

  SP    Sushant Prints
  26    the year
  07    the month
  0042  the forty second order that month

The count starts again at 0001 on the first of every month.
Two people booking at the same moment cannot get the same number.

================================================================
OPTIONAL: NIGHTLY BACKUP TO GOOGLE SHEETS
================================================================
Every night at midnight the app can copy the day's orders into a
Google Sheet, one tab per date. To switch it on, add these three
Railway variables:

  GOOGLE_SHEET_ID       from your Google Sheet URL
  GOOGLE_CLIENT_EMAIL   from the service account JSON
  GOOGLE_PRIVATE_KEY    from the service account JSON

Then use "Run backup now" in Settings to check it works.
Ask your developer to set up the Google service account.

================================================================
USING IT ON A PHONE
================================================================
1. Open the link in Chrome on Android, or Safari on iPhone
2. Sign in once. It stays signed in for thirty days.
3. Open the browser menu and choose Add to Home Screen.
   It then opens like a normal app.

The camera only works over https, which your Railway link already
is, so scanning works out of the box.

================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
================================================================
App will not load
     Check the Railway dashboard. Is the service green?

"DATABASE_URL not set"
     Step 2 was missed. Add the PostgreSQL database.

Camera will not open
     Android needs Chrome, iPhone needs Safari. Check that the
     browser has camera permission in the phone settings.

Print window does not appear
     The browser blocked the pop-up. Allow pop-ups for the site
     and press Print again.

Scanned QR says no order found
     The form was probably printed from a different domain than
     the one you are on now. Type the order number into the Scan
     tab instead. It will find it.

Forgot the password
     Railway, Variables, change APP_PASSWORD, redeploy.

Build failed
     Send the Railway build log to your developer.
